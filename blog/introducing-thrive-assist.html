Introducing Thrive Assist: Empowering Independence

Discover our new accessibility-first platform connecting Persons with Disabilities with trusted helpers, volunteers, and sign language interpreters in real-time.

By ThriveFusion Team

We are thrilled to announce the launch of Thrive Assist, our flagship platform designed to empower Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) by connecting them with a trusted network of volunteers, scribes, sign language interpreters, caregivers, and mobility assistants.

Why We Built Thrive Assist

Accessing reliable, real-time assistance has long been a challenge for many in our community. Whether it’s requesting a sign language interpreter for a last-minute meeting, finding a scribe for an examination, or simply connecting with a mentor, the process was often fragmented and difficult to navigate.

Thrive Assist changes that by bringing everything into one accessible, intuitive ecosystem.

Key Features

  • Real-Time Requests: Need assistance right now? Our location-aware matching system connects you with available volunteers nearby.
  • Trusted Network: Every volunteer, interpreter, and caregiver undergoes a verification process to ensure safety and quality of support.
  • Accessibility First: Built from the ground up to exceed WCAG 2.2 AA standards, ensuring full compatibility with screen readers and assistive technologies.
  • Secure Messaging: Coordinate seamlessly through our encrypted communication channels.

Getting Started

Thrive Assist is now available for early access. If you are a Person with a Disability seeking support, or an individual looking to volunteer your time and skills, we invite you to join our growing community.

Together, we can build a world where independence is empowered through connected communities.
