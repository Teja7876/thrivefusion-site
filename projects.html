Thrive Assist

Empowering independence through connected communities.

An accessibility-first platform connecting persons with disabilities with Scribes, Volunteers, Sign Language Interpreters, Readers, Caregivers, and other accessibility support.

Key Features

  • Real-time assistance requests
  • Sign language interpreter & scribe network
  • Location-aware helper matching
  • Secure messaging & scheduling

EqualEdge AI

Authoritative intelligence for accessibility and rights.

An AI-powered platform that provides reliable and authoritative information about disabilities, accessibility, government schemes, disability rights, laws, education, employment, assistive technology, accessibility standards, and inclusive resources using trusted official sources.

Key Features

  • Verified guidance on government schemes & laws
  • Disability rights & legal frameworks information
  • Assistive technology & standards recommendations
  • Inclusive education & employment resources

Meatly

Accessible communication and collaboration for everyone.

An accessibility-first communication and collaboration platform designed as an accessible alternative to traditional online meeting platforms with outstanding screen-reader support, keyboard accessibility, and inclusive collaboration.

Key Features

  • Screen reader & keyboard first design
  • Live captions & real-time transcription
  • Sign language video pin & spotlight
  • Inclusive collaboration & meeting tools
