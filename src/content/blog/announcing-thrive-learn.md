We are proud to unveil **Thrive Learn**, an innovative eLearning platform dedicated to providing inclusive, barrier-free education for Persons with Disabilities (PwDs).

Education is a fundamental right, yet many traditional online learning environments remain difficult or impossible to use for individuals with visual, cognitive, or physical disabilities. We built Thrive Learn to bridge this gap.

## A New Era of Inclusive Learning

Thrive Learn integrates cutting-edge assistive technologies directly into the learning experience, eliminating the need for expensive third-party tools.

### Core Features

- **AI Learning Assistance:** Get personalized tutoring, simplified explanations, and adaptive pacing tailored to your unique cognitive and learning needs.
- **Built-in OCR & Document Readers:** Instantly convert inaccessible PDFs and images into structured, screen-reader-friendly text.
- **Accessible Assessments:** Quizzes and exams designed with flexible time limits, keyboard navigation, and alternative input methods.
- **Progress Tracking & Certification:** Earn recognized certificates to support your career advancement in a supportive, accessible environment.

## Empowering Careers

Thrive Learn isn't just about taking courses; it's about preparing for the future. By offering accessible skills training and certification, we aim to drastically improve the employment prospects and independence of our community members.

Thrive Learn is currently in development and will be launching soon.
