In a world increasingly reliant on remote communication, we noticed a glaring gap: popular video conferencing tools often treat accessibility as an afterthought. From confusing keyboard navigation to unreliable captions and poor support for sign language interpreters, these platforms frequently leave Persons with Disabilities feeling excluded.

That's why we built **Meatly**.

## Designed for Inclusion

Meatly isn't just another video conferencing tool; it is an accessible-first platform designed to ensure that everyone has an equal seat at the virtual table.

### What Makes Meatly Different?

1. **Screen Reader Optimization:** Every button, menu, and alert in Meatly is meticulously labeled and structured for perfect compatibility with popular screen readers like NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver.
2. **Keyboard Accessibility:** Navigate the entire platform, manage your microphone and camera, and participate in chat using only your keyboard—no mouse required.
3. **Sign Language Pinning:** Ensure your interpreter is always visible, regardless of who is speaking. Meatly provides dedicated layout options specifically for sign language users.
4. **Live Captions & AI Transcripts:** Powered by advanced AI, Meatly delivers highly accurate, multilingual live captions and instant transcriptions to make meetings accessible to the Deaf and Hard of Hearing community.

## Try Meatly Today

We believe that remote work and virtual collaboration should be barrier-free. Meatly is currently rolling out to early adopters. Experience the difference of an inclusive platform today.
